# CodeIQ Bills

We are excited to introduce **CodeIQ Bills**, the centralized billing engine! This script allows you to manage and process invoices from multiple sources in one unified interface, providing flexibility and scalability for your server’s billing management.

## Features

The **CodeIQ Bills** system offers:

- **Centralized Management**: Process and query invoices from multiple external resources in one engine.
- **Customizable Handlers**: Register handlers to process bills for specific sources.
- **SQL Integration**: Flexible querying and updating of your invoice tables.
- **Ease of Use**: Simple configuration for adding, modifying, and managing new invoice sources.
- **Extensibility**: Compatible with custom resources and systems like `origen_masterjob` or `origen_police`.

<Callout type="info">
    Properly configured databases and billing tables are required for each external source.

    All invoice processing and querying are handled through one centralized engine, simplifying the management of multiple data sources.
</Callout>
