## Origen Masterjob Injection

<Callout type="info">
    This is essential to list the jobs from `origen_masterjob` on the commerces tab.
</Callout>

You must insert the following line at the bottom of the `fxmanifest.lua` file in the `origen_masterjob` resource:

```lua filename="fxmanifest.lua"
server_script  "@cq-lib/injections/server/origen_masterjob.lua"
```

Also be sure that the script start next to the cq-lib on your server.cfg, like this:

```cfg filename="server.cfg"
ensure cq-lib
ensure origen_masterjob
ensure cq-bills
ensure cq-pausemenu
```
